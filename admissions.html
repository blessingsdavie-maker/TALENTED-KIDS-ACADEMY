Admissions

Four simple steps to joining TALENTED KIDS ACADEMY.

We keep the process personal — a real conversation, a school visit, and a straightforward form. No hidden steps.

1

Reach out

Call, email, or send the form below and tell us your child's age and the grade you're applying for.

2

Visit a class

We'll set up a weekday morning visit so your child can sit in on a real class and meet a teacher.

3

Submit documents

Birth certificate, immunisation record, and a copy of the most recent report card (if applicable).

4

Confirm your spot

Once your place is confirmed, we'll send a welcome pack with supply lists and first-day details.
