Academics & Programs

A curriculum that grows in step with your child.

From Playgroup through Grade 6, our Competency-Based Curriculum guides every learner through skills mastery, real work, and meaningful reflection so children progress with confidence.

Competency-Based Learning

Skills that matter at every stage

We anchor teaching in clear, age-appropriate competencies: literacy, numeracy, inquiry, social skills, and personal growth. This helps children from Playgroup to Grade 6 know the next goal and own their learning.

Kindergarten is play-first and language-rich. Children build early literacy and number sense through stories, songs, blocks, and guided discovery — with plenty of time outdoors.

Literacy

Letters & Sounds

Phonics through song, story time, and mark-making.

Numeracy

Numbers to 20

Counting, patterns, and shape play with hands-on materials.

Discovery

My World

Seasons, senses, and simple science through observation.

Creative

Art & Movement

Daily art, music, and gross-motor play.

Grades 1–2 introduce structured literacy and math blocks, while keeping mornings hands-on and afternoons open for art, music, and outdoor learning.

Literacy

Reading & Writing

Guided reading groups and daily independent writing.

Numeracy

Addition & Place Value

Mental math strategies alongside concrete manipulatives.

Science

Living Things

Simple experiments and a class garden plot each spring.

Social Studies

Our Community

Maps, neighbourhoods, and local field trips.

Enrichment

Music & PE

Two sessions weekly of each, plus weekly library time.

Grades 3–6 build toward middle school readiness: longer projects, more independent research, and an introduction to a second language.

Literacy

Reading & Composition

Novel studies, essay structure, and peer editing.

Numeracy

Fractions to Pre-Algebra

Problem-solving blocks with weekly math challenges.

Science

Investigations

Hypothesis-driven projects, culminating in a spring science fair.

Language

Intro Spanish

Conversational Spanish, three sessions a week.

Enrichment

Art, Music & ICT

Rotating enrichment blocks plus a Grade 5 coding unit.

Leadership

Grade 6 Projects

Independent projects that build responsibility, research, and presentation skills.
