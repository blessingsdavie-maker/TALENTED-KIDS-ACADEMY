Our story

About TALENTED KIDS ACADEMY

The story of the school began in 2006, when it was established as a humble ECDE centre with only seven students, marking the beginning of a remarkable journey of growth and transformation.Today we serve over 200 children from Playgroup through Grade 6, but we've kept the same idea at our core: school should feel warm, safe, and genuinely exciting to walk into.

  • Curiosity first. We'd rather a child ask a great question than memorise a fast answer.
  • Every child known. Small classes mean no one gets lost in the back row.
  • Kindness is a skill. We teach it as deliberately as we teach reading.
“We don't just prepare children for the next grade. We try to send them on with a genuine love of learning — that's the part that lasts.” — Mr. Silvanus Isindu, Director
